ZEIT Prototype Bench

The complete prototype in three dimensions — the three-microphone headset, the audio interface, the Raspberry Pi 5 that runs the engine, and the communication unit the result is delivered to. No development computer sits in the signal path. Fire a shot and the signal is traced through every stage: the wavefronts propagate, the microphones respond, the eleven blocks execute, and every event is written to a timestamped log that stays on screen.
