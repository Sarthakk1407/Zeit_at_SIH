ANVIL Prototype Bench

The locked prototype in three dimensions, laid out as it is actually built and wired: the six-microphone headset on a measurement mannequin, the preamplifier, the ADAU1787 that runs the ear, the STM32 that manages it, the amplifier, the UMC404HD, the Jetson Orin Nano Super that runs the neural chain, and the radio it all ends at. Every part is modelled at the size its datasheet gives.

Both lanes run on this page. Underneath the bench, Lane A and Lane B execute as real signal-processing code at 16 kHz in 16 ms frames — the hear-through filter, the level-dependent gain, the crack guard, the limiters and a filtered-x LMS canceller; then framing, an STFT, a noise mask and the throat-microphone voice guard. The monitor, the meters and the firmware log below are showing that computation, not an animation of it.
