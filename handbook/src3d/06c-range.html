The headset keeps its bench size, ten times too large for the range, because at true size it would be a speck. The viewport says so. The crack. The run takes the crack as leaving the round's path at closest approach and crossing the miss distance at the speed of sound. That is a cone of half-angle atan(c/v) = 22.7 degrees; the textbook Mach cone, asin(c/v), is 24.7 degrees and would arrive 0.8 ms sooner.
