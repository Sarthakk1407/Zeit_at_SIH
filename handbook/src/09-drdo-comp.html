What DRDO actually wants

A sponsor read. Partly inference from how DRDO and its funding schemes operate rather than a published SIH rubric — treat it as a lens, not a checklist.

DRDO is a government R&D organisation under the Department of Defence Production. Its institutional instinct is qualification, not demonstration. A working demo answers "does it do something". DRDO's question is "can this be specified, tested, reproduced, made in India, and fielded by someone who has never met you."

It thinks in Technology Readiness Levels

DRDO's Technology Development Fund exists specifically to raise technologies "from TRL 3 onwards" to tri-services specifications, and iDEX Prime targets TRL 4 and above with grants up to ₹10 crore. A hackathon entry is TRL 3–4.

The winning move is not to pretend you are at TRL 7 — it is to say precisely where you are and what the next two levels cost. A slide reading "we are at TRL 4; TRL 5 requires environmental qualification per JSS 55555 and a 40-hour field trial; here is the plan" reads as competence. A slide claiming a deployable soldier system reads as naïve.

It thinks in standards and limits, not benchmarks

An acoustics evaluator has MIL-STD-1474 in their head, not VCTK-DEMAND. That standard requires impulse peak levels below 140 dB at the ear, protected or unprotected — while few weapons produce below 150 dB and many exceed 180. That is the frame your project will be heard in, and your measured 140–155 dB muzzle blast figure lands inside a standard they already know.

Equally: to a defence evaluator, "speech intelligibility" has a formal meaning — ANSI/ASA S3.2, measured by Diagnostic Rhyme Test, Modified Rhyme Test, or phonetically balanced word lists, with human listeners. PESQ and STOI are proxies for that. You do not need to run a DRT, but naming it as the next rung shows you know there is a ladder.

It cares about indigenisation and IP

"Make in India" and Atmanirbhar Bharat are procurement policy here, not slogans. Permissive licences, no GPL, no cloud dependency, and a clear statement of what is yours are worth real marks. Our PFFFT-over-FFTW decision was made on exactly these grounds and should be said out loud. Conversely, note that DNSMOS is an Azure service — a foreign-cloud evaluation dependency is worth a sentence of acknowledgement and an offline fallback.

It rewards honest negative results

This is where the project is unusually well positioned. The gap analysis is exactly the register DRDO respects: naming where each published method stops, then measuring the collapse. "Deep ANC was never tested on the noise class our sponsor names first, and here is what happens when you do" is a stronger DRDO story than a polished demo.

The same goes for the provenance discipline. Absolute calibration, a holdout assigned before data exists, and a frozen reference that checksums the measurement engine itself are what qualification looks like at student scale.

It will ask what breaks in the field

Temperature, dust, vibration, fungus, EMI, power, and what happens when a component fails. JSS 55555 is the Indian joint-services environmental specification; MIL-STD-810 is the international analogue. You will not run these tests, but knowing they exist and naming which ones would apply is the difference between a student project and a proposal.

The operational hook worth stating early

Reporting from SOF Week 2026 raised questions about the lack of standardised tactical hearing protection among Indian special forces, and the Indian Army has only recently begun inducting DRDO-designed software-defined radios built by BEL. Your device sits precisely between those two facts: a headset that protects hearing and feeds an indigenous radio. One sentence, early, converts an academic project into a capability gap.

What the median competing team will have

This is a well-trodden path and it is genuinely quick, which is why most of the five to seven teams will be on it:

  • A pretrained denoiser — DeepFilterNet, RNNoise, or a DNS-Challenge baseline — fine-tuned for a few epochs.
  • Gunshot clips pulled from ESC-50, UrbanSound8K or FreeSound, mixed with LibriSpeech at a handful of SNRs.
  • PESQ and STOI numbers, frequently quoted from the source paper rather than measured on their own run.
  • A live laptop demo: speak into a mic, play a gunshot from a speaker, toggle the denoiser on and off. It will sound impressive.
  • A Jetson, if the college owns one, because the PS names it.
  • A polished deck with an architecture diagram that does not distinguish active noise control from speech enhancement.

Where that team is weak

  • No calibration — every level is relative, so no claim can be compared to physics or to anyone else's measurement.
  • No holdout discipline — tuned and evaluated on the same data, and unable to answer "how do we know this generalises".
  • No latency or power measured on real hardware; "real-time" asserted from a laptop.
  • Cannot answer "what happens at −10 dB SNR", "what does your denoiser do to the vocoder", or "which of your numbers is Lane A and which is Lane B".
  • Trained on neutral read speech and demonstrated with someone speaking calmly — while the actual use case is a person shouting under fire.
  • No error bounds, no negative results, nothing a reviewer can audit.

Where we are genuinely ahead

  1. Measured, calibrated, holdout-split, checksum-frozen impulsive data. Anyone can download ten thousand gunshots this afternoon — the Cadre forensics set alone has that many. Nobody else will have absolute-SPL recordings with a measured range impulse response and a frozen measurement engine. State the claim that precisely and it becomes unassailable rather than merely bold.
  2. A gap analysis instead of a literature review. Naming where each published method stops is a different register from summarising what it does, and it is the register DRDO respects.
  3. Correct two-lane architecture. Most teams will imply a neural network does their active cancellation. Being visibly right about the physics costs nothing and separates you immediately.
  4. A SWaP-C argument. 23.7 K parameters runs on a microcontroller. Saying "the PS suggests a Jetson AGX Orin; we do not need one, and here is the measured power draw" reframes the whole conversation for a soldier-worn device.
  5. Reproducibility as engineering practice — frozen references, provenance guards, seeded configs, a parity test. This is what qualification looks like, and it is what separates a project from a demo.
  6. Two metrics nobody else has, IRT and WLPS, plus the pre/post-vocoder comparison. All three cost days, not weeks.

Where we are genuinely behind

Be honest about this internally

We have no working prototype, no trained model, no named board, and no measured latency. The median team will have a demo that works in the room. A rough working thing beats an excellent document at a 36-hour hardware finale — judges are explicitly told to expect a working prototype, not a deck. Every strength listed above only counts if there is something plugged in beside it.

The realistic threat is not a team with better research. It is a team from a college with an acoustics lab, or a team that simply got a board running early and spent the remaining weeks polishing the demo while we were reading papers.

The one claim to rewrite before it reaches a slide — audit F3

The capture plan calls the geometric look-ahead "the strongest single idea", and it is printed across the bottom of the flagship architecture diagram: Mic 2 hears the blast ~29 ms early at 10 m. That is true of the recording rig, where Mic 2 is deliberately placed at the gun. It is not true of the product, where Mic 2 sits on the earcup about 20 cm from Mic 1 — giving 0.6 ms. The same diagram shows both facts, so a judge reading it left to right finds the contradiction inside the diagram.

There is a real, deployable version: a supersonic round's ballistic crack always arrives before the muzzle blast — tens of milliseconds of genuine physical look-ahead at a single mic pair on one head, defensible from the counter-sniper literature. Keep the geometric version as what it honestly is, a property of the data-collection design that measures the source-to-speaker transfer function directly, and let the crack carry the deployment claim.
