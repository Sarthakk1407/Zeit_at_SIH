Two things to do out of order

  • Implement IRT and WLPS before the range trip. About fifty lines each, no hardware, no range data, no trained model. If the range trip slips, you still have a research contribution.
  • Build the acoustic-path measurement tool before the hardware exists. Sweep → deconvolution → impulse response. The hardware side will need it the day the first prototype exists, and ir_extract.py already does most of it.

Hardware-matched training — the real co-design argument

Once a prototype headset exists: measure its primary path (reference mic → error mic) and secondary path (speaker → error mic) by log sweep and deconvolution, feed those measured impulse responses into the mixing engine, and retrain. The model is then matched to your specific hardware, and no competing team can replicate it without your physical device. That is what "hardware-software co-design" actually means, and it directly serves the PS's prototype-demonstration deliverable.

What is missing from the research, and what it blocks

  1. Gunshot and blast acoustics — deliberately ours, measured not cited. That decision stands and is a genuine differentiator. But three narrow things do not come from measuring: (a) one external reference figure for expected peak SPL and duration by source type, so a 118 dB reading can be told apart from a saturating capsule; (b) a citation for the Friedlander functional form already in make_test_data.py, which is currently the one physical assumption under the whole generator with no source behind it; (c) a basis for extrapolating beyond the measured envelope. Get those and the measured data becomes stronger, because it can be shown to agree with — or defensibly differ from — a published expectation.
  2. Outdoor sound propagation — nothing yet. Recording at two distances specifically so the generator can be tested on propagation, with no source on spreading loss, air absorption versus temperature and humidity, or ground effect. Blocks generator step 3.1.
  3. Room impulse response simulation — nothing yet. Stage 4 calls for a wide spread of RIRs; we have one measured range IR and no method for simulating the rest.
  4. Shao & Nikias is cited second-hand. FLOM is scheduled to ship on the device as block 3, but the paper is known only through Yuan et al. Get the primary source before implementing a statistic in C++ from a paraphrase.
  5. Two papers in the folder are uncited — Le Roux on SI-SDR, which fixes the metrics argument, and DNSMOS P.835, which is still credited to a screenshot.

What is unverified in this document

Read this before putting any number on a slide

Every published figure here was read from the converted Markdown copies in docs/research/, and table structure did not always survive that conversion cleanly. In particular, verify Deep ANC's Table 1 cells — the −11.07 / −9.60 / −7.93 NMSE figures anchoring the latency argument — against the original PDF, and confirm you are reading the engine-noise linear row.

No claim in this document has been reproduced experimentally by us. GTCRN's parameter count is reported two different ways by two sources; both are quoted deliberately. And the sizing, allocation and timing figures for the range trip are plans, not outcomes.

The project's own rule applies: if a number in the notes disagrees with the paper, the paper is right.

What is already right — protect these from any rewrite

The robust-normalisation argument

RMS is a second moment; for α-stable noise with α < 2 the variance is infinite. A real, explainable defect in the closest prior system, with the fix cited inside another paper you already hold.

Freezing the measurement engine, not just the data

SHA-256 of analyze.py, dsp.py and wavio.py alongside every WAV. Almost nobody does this. It answers "how do we know your reference did not drift" before it is asked.

Assigning fit/holdout at planning time

Written into the metadata CSV before any data exists. Choosing it afterwards is precisely the circularity it prevents — F12 is a sizing fix, not an argument against the discipline.

The B + C ≈ A additivity check

Voice-only plus gun-only against both-at-once, all three real recordings. Tests additive mixing, the clipping model and SNR bookkeeping at once, for the cost of discipline at the range.

Refusing lossy audio — with measurements

Mid-band within 0.1 dB, but the 32 kHz band gone at −83 dB and the centroid shifted 248 Hz against a real σ of 1.43 Hz. A 173× shift. That is how to justify a format decision.

Licence discipline

PFFFT over FFTW on GPL grounds; no libsndfile so a broken native library cannot strand you at the range. Both correct, and both worth saying out loud to this sponsor.

The gap analysis itself

Naming where each published method stops, rather than summarising what it does, is what separates this from every other entry. The α-stable clipping inversion is the single best idea in the pack.

The real-time rules

No allocation, no locks, no exceptions, no logging in the audio callback — written down before a line of C++ exists. Student teams almost never do this, and the bug it prevents is nearly undebuggable.
