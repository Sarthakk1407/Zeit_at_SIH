Note to the evaluator

This document separates three things that are easily conflated, and labels each one throughout: what has been built and tested, what has been designed but not yet built, and what is quoted from published work rather than reproduced by us. Section 02 gives the honest inventory; every figure taken from a paper is attributed at the point of use, and Section 17 lists what remains unverified.

Where our own analysis found a defect in this project's design, it is stated in place rather than omitted — those passages are marked with a finding number such as F1 and are set out in full in the companion Architecture Audit.

How to read

Ten numbered figures carry the architecture. Figure 1 is the whole system on one page; Figures 2 and 4 are the two lanes in full; Figure 3 is the timing the design depends on. If you read nothing else, read those four. Any word with a dotted underline is a technical term. Hover it, or tap it on a phone, for a plain-language definition and how it is used in this project specifically. There is no glossary to scroll to; the explanation comes to the word.

Interactive companion ZEIT Prototype Bench A three-dimensional model of the complete prototype, with both lanes modelled. The earcup is drawn cut away so the anti-noise driver, the error microphone and the Lane A filter module are visible; a lane filter isolates either system on its own. Fire a simulated shot to watch the wavefronts propagate, Lane A close its loop inside a millisecond, and the eleven Lane B blocks execute against their 25.3 ms budget beside a four-channel signal monitor.
